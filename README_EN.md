### Open Source Projects

- [Wenku8Downloader](https://github.com/mj3622/Wenku8Downloader)<br>
  A desktop app for searching and downloading light novels, exporting individual volumes as EPUB files, saving illustrations, and managing download tasks.

- [NoveliaReminder](https://github.com/mj3622/NoveliaReminder)<br>
  Checks followed novels on a schedule and sends an email or Feishu notification when a new chapter appears.

- [EPUB Merger](https://github.com/mj3622/epub-merger)<br>
  Reorders and merges EPUB books entirely in the browser, so files never need to leave the user's device.

- [Simplified-Traditional Converter](https://github.com/mj3622/simplified-traditional-converter)<br>
  Batch converts TXT and EPUB files from Traditional Chinese to Simplified Chinese.
